Responsible AI & Clinical Governance

Core architectural guardrails ensuring medical safety, zero hallucinations, and auditability

Deterministic Reference Bounds

MedLens strictly forbids AI models from generating, estimating, or looking up standard reference ranges. All interval comparisons are executed by a pure deterministic mathematical evaluator against text explicitly printed in the uploaded report.

Zero Medical Diagnosis Guarantee

Prompt templates enforce strict negative constraints prohibiting disease diagnostic terms, medication dosage proposals, or clinical management instructions.

End-to-End Source Provenance

Every clinical data point maintains an immutable audit pointer containing the filename, page number, bounding coordinates, and verbatim source snippet.

Human-in-the-Loop Review

Extracted biomarkers are routed through a dedicated validation queue, allowing clinicians or patients to verify, correct, or reject data points before integration.
